About Discount Beds Belfast

A Belfast bed shop with over 20 years of experience in bed and mattress retail.

Who we are

Discount Beds Belfast is a bed store at 8 Templemore Avenue, Belfast. We have over 20 years of experience in bed and mattress retail, and we aim to keep our customers happy, because customers are the key to a good business.

What we sell

Our range includes mattresses (memory foam and pocket sprung), divan beds, upholstered and leather beds, ottoman bed frames, headboards, children's beds, bunk beds and sofa beds. You can browse the range and prices by category.

Delivery across Northern Ireland

We deliver throughout Northern Ireland, and offer free local same-day or next-day delivery on beds and mattresses in stock.

Landlords

We're already the preferred supplier to many established landlords in Belfast and surrounding areas. If you're furnishing several properties, ask us about a landlord order.

Talk to our team

Our sales managers, Neill and Sandra, are happy to give you advice. Tell us what you need or call 028 9045 3723.
