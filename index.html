How much is delivery?

Local delivery is free, same day or next day, on beds and mattresses we have in stock. We also deliver across all six counties, including Bangor, Lisburn, Newtownards, Carrickfergus, Ballymena, Antrim, Portadown, Newry, Omagh and Coleraine. Give us your postcode and we'll confirm the cost before you order.

Is the bed assembled?

Bed frames arrive flatpack for self-assembly. It's worth checking you have a screwdriver and an extra pair of hands.

How do deposits work?

A deposit secures your item. We either hold it in stock for you or order it in, paying the supplier in full, so deposits are non-refundable and non-transferable.

What if something arrives damaged?

Let us know within 48 hours of delivery and we'll replace damaged or missing parts as quickly as possible. Please keep every part, as anything thrown away can't be replaced free of charge.

Can I return a bed that doesn't fit?

Please check room sizes before you order. We're happy to give you exact measurements for any item first. We refund if we can't replace or fix a bed within a reasonable time, or if your item is discontinued or out of stock longer than agreed.
