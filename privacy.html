Who we are

Discount Beds Belfast, 8 Templemore Avenue, Belfast BT5 4FP, is responsible for the personal details you give us through this website. Contact us on 028 9045 3723 or discountbedsbelfast@yahoo.co.uk.

What we collect and why

  • Callback and reservation forms: your name, phone number or email, the product you're interested in and any message. We use these only to reply to your enquiry and arrange your order. Our lawful basis is taking steps at your request before entering into a contract.
  • Where you came from: if you arrived from one of our adverts, the form also records which advert, so we know which ones are worth paying for.
  • Phone calls: we may show a different phone number depending on which advert you came from. These numbers forward to our shop and let us count calls from each advert. Call tracking services may record the time, length and number of the call.

We never sell or share your details for marketing. We keep enquiry details for no longer than 12 months unless you place an order, in which case we keep order records as required for tax and warranty purposes.

Cookies

  • Analytics cookies (optional): Google Analytics, only if you click Accept. It tells us which pages help people find the right bed. We don't use advertising cookies.
  • Remembering you (on your device only): if you fill in a form, your browser remembers your name and number so you don't have to type them again. This stays on your device and you can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the details we hold about you, or object to how we use them. Just call or email us. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
